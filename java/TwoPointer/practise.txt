Great—here are 10 Google-level Two Pointers problems, grouped by pattern, with why they matter and what to focus on (no full solutions so you can practice).

⸻

🧱 1. Opposite Direction (Left–Right)

⸻

1️⃣ Two Sum II

Pattern: Opposite pointers
Why important:

Classic sorted array pruning
Foundation of many problems

Focus:

Move left/right based on sum
Explain WHY movement is correct


⸻

2️⃣ Container With Most Water ⭐

Pattern: Opposite pointers + Greedy
Why Google likes it:

Requires reasoning, not brute force

Focus:

Why move the shorter side only
Proof of correctness


⸻

⚙️ 2. Fast–Slow Pointer (In-place / Linked List)

⸻

3️⃣ Remove Duplicates from Sorted Array

Pattern: Fast–slow
Focus:

Write index vs read index
In-place overwrite


⸻

4️⃣ Linked List Cycle ⭐

Pattern: Fast–slow (Floyd)
Why important:

Classic pointer math question

Focus:

Why fast=2x catches slow


⸻

🔁 3. Same Direction (Matching / Subsequence)

⸻

5️⃣ Is Subsequence

Pattern: Same direction
Focus:

One pointer matches, one scans


⸻

6️⃣ Backspace String Compare ⭐

Pattern: Reverse two pointers
Why Google likes it:

Requires simulation + pointer trick

Focus:

Process from right to left
Skip logic


⸻

🔀 4. Partition / Rearrangement

⸻

7️⃣ Sort Colors ⭐

Pattern: 3-way partition
Why important:

Dutch National Flag problem

Focus:

3 pointers: low, mid, high
Maintain invariant regions


⸻

8️⃣ Move Zeroes

Pattern: Stable partition
Focus:

Keep relative order
Swap vs overwrite trade-off


⸻

🔗 5. Merge Pattern

⸻

9️⃣ Merge Sorted Array ⭐

Pattern: Merge (reverse pointers)
Why important:

In-place + backward pointers

Focus:

Fill from end to avoid overwrite


⸻

🔢 6. K-Sum (Fix + Two Pointers)

⸻

🔟 3Sum ⭐⭐⭐

Pattern: Sorting + two pointers
Why Google LOVES it:

Combines:
sorting
deduplication
pointer logic

Focus:

Skip duplicates correctly
Fix i → solve 2Sum

🎯 How to practice (very important)

For each problem, train yourself to answer:

1. Why can we use two pointers?
2. What invariant do we maintain?
3. Why pointer movement is correct?
4. What is the time complexity?


⸻

🔥 Most important takeaway

Google doesn’t test if you know Two Pointers—
they test if you can justify pointer movement and invariants.

## 💡 关键学习建议

### ✅ 应该做的
1. **有计划地学习** - 按周次顺序，不要跳过
2. **深度优于广度** - 一个模式 3 道题，彻底理解
3. **自己标注框架** - 看别人的注释后，关闭文件自己写
4. **写代码就写** - 不要看答案，自己实现
5. **定期回顾** - 每周做一次过去的题

### ❌ 不应该做的
1. **随机刷题** - 这样无法建立系统思维
2. **只看不写** - 复制粘贴等于没有学
3. **追求数量** - 10 道题透彻 > 100 道题懵懂
4. **放弃调试** - 错了就琢磨，不要看答案
5. **忽视框架** - 框架是核心，代码只是表达

## 📝 常见问题 FAQ

**Q: 如果一周没有完成进度怎么办？**  
A: 不要赶进度，重点是理解。可延长到下周，但保持学习的连贯性。

**Q: 某个模式一直不理解怎么办？**  
A: 尝试不同的学习方式：看更多例题、写伪代码、与人讨论。

**Q: 需要背模板吗？**  
A: 不需要背，理解逻辑后自然能写。无法写出，说明理解不足。

**Q: 可以调整学习顺序吗？**  
A: 前 4 周的顺序很重要（从易到难），后面可灵活调整。

## 💡 Key Learning Tips

### ✅ Do's
1. **Plan your learning** - Follow week sequence, don't skip
2. **Depth over breadth** - 3 problems per pattern, understand thoroughly
3. **Self-annotate** - After seeing annotations, close file and write yourself
4. **Always code** - Don't look at solutions, implement yourself
5. **Review regularly** - One review session per week

### ❌ Don'ts
1. **Random problem solving** - Won't build systematic thinking
2. **Just reading** - Copying = not learning
3. **Quantity focus** - 10 thorough problems > 100 confused ones
4. **Skip debugging** - Figure out mistakes, don't look at answers
5. **Ignore framework** - Framework is core, code is expression

## 📝 FAQ

**Q: What if I can't complete a week's progress?**  
A: Don't rush. Focus on understanding. You can extend to next week but maintain learning continuity.

**Q: I still don't understand a pattern. What should I do?**  
A: Try different learning approaches: more examples, pseudocode, discuss with others.

**Q: Do I need to memorize templates?**  
A: No need to memorize. After understanding, you'll naturally write them. If you can't, you don't understand yet.

**Q: Can I change the learning order?**  
A: First 4 weeks' order is important (easy to hard). After that, flexible adjustments are OK.
